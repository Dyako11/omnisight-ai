The Global Challenge & Comprehensive Solution

The Global Challenge

Over 43 million people worldwide live with total blindness, while 295 million suffer from severe visual impairments. Traditional assistive tools like white canes and guide dogs leave critical safety gaps:

  • Hazardous Blind Spots: White canes only detect ground obstacles, missing overhead branches, poles, signboards, and open windows that cause severe facial trauma.
  • Lack of Spatial & Context Awareness: Canes cannot read street signs, identify currency notes, describe bank cards, parse menus, or identify social cues.
  • Physical Isolation: Constantly relying on external assistance breeds isolation and fear during everyday navigation.

The Complete OmniSight AI Architecture

OmniSight AI combines lightweight ergonomic hardware with generative AI to serve as continuous, real-time digital sight:

  • 360° Vertical Convex Camera: Mounted seamlessly on the bridge to scan 180° vertically (sky to ground) and 180° horizontally, capturing curbs, steps, and overhead hazards simultaneously without turning the head.
  • Tactile Frame Controls: Features physical buttons for Quick Description Mode (instant hazard alerts for busy sidewalks) and Detailed Description Mode (rich environmental narrative, colors, faces, and emotional context).
  • Dedicated Power & Emergency SOS Buttons: Holding the tactile SOS button instantly broadcasts real-time GPS coordinates and 360° environment snapshots to loved ones and emergency contacts.
  • Open-Ear Directional Audio & Microphone: Dual directional temple speakers deliver clear AI audio prompts without covering ear canals, maintaining ambient sound awareness. An integrated noise-canceling microphone enables conversational multilingual interaction.
  • Smartphone Management & Offline AI Engine: Syncs via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi to handle calls, announce callers, read incoming messages, and provide step-by-step turn navigation. Includes an offline vision core for continuous obstacle scanning without cellular connectivity.
  • Shopping & Financial Assistant: Instantly scans product barcodes, reads expiration dates, identifies bank cards, and verifies physical paper currency values during payments.

Core Technical Pillars

360° Vertical Coverage

Convex optics continuously monitor low steps and high overhead hazards in parallel without head adjustment.

Dual Perception Core

Switch between rapid obstacle pings or rich contextual environmental narration with single tactile button presses.

Open-Ear Directional Audio

Pipes crystal-clear spatial audio cues into the ear canal while keeping environmental hearing fully open.

Ergonomic Hardware Architecture

Engineered with physical tactile buttons, emergency SOS triggers, noise-canceling microphone, and a lightweight opaque frame.
