Core AI Capabilities

360° Vertical Camera

Eliminates blind spots completely. Simultaneously scans low obstacles (curbs, steps) and high hazards (hanging branches, overhead poles) up to 180° vertically and horizontally.

Dual AI Perception Engine

Offers two modes: "Quick Description" for swift navigation alerts and "Detailed Description" for deep scene understanding, colors, textures, and face identification.

Open-Ear Directional Audio

Dual directional speakers near the temples deliver crystal-clear audio cues without blocking natural ear canals, maintaining complete environmental hearing safety.

Ergonomic Hardware Architecture

Designed with tactile controls, emergency SOS triggers, pinhole noise-canceling microphone, and a lightweight opaque frame.

Driven by Public Good Impact

Building accessible, open-source-assisted technology to deliver independence and safety at cost-price manufacturing.
