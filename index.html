Core Project Objective

Currently, over 43 million people worldwide suffer from complete blindness, and 295 million live with severe visual impairments. The OmniSight AI project aims to completely eliminate the physical dependency of blind individuals on those around them, establish flawless safety in urban navigation, and deliver live, immediate digital vision — so that no blind person remains confined to solitude and absolute darkness.

2. Problem Statement

Imagine waking up every morning, opening your eyes, and seeing nothing but absolute darkness. For every step you take on the street, for navigating a staircase, for reading a café menu, for identifying the banknote in your hand, or even for knowing whether the person in front of you is smiling — you need someone else's help.

Traditional tools like the white cane or guide dogs have barely changed in decades and carry critical limitations:

  • Dangerous blind spots: The white cane only detects obstacles close to the ground. It never detects overhanging tree branches, horizontal building bars, shop awnings, urban signage, or mailboxes at head level — leading to severe collisions and serious physical injuries.
  • Lack of environmental understanding and description: A cane or guide dog cannot read street signs, display phone messages, describe the color of clothing, identify a bank card, or describe the program playing on television.
  • A sense of dependency and isolation: These limitations leave blind individuals constantly feeling at risk, fearing getting lost, and dependent on others for the simplest daily tasks.

3. Complete Solution Overview and Glasses Specifications

OmniSight AI glasses integrate all of these challenges into an exceptionally lightweight, ergonomic, modern, and elegant frame. These glasses function as a pair of intelligent, alert eyes that perceive the environment precisely and communicate everything to the user in the calmest and clearest way possible.

  • 1. Ultra-Wide 360° Vertical Camera System: Positioned precisely at the bridge of the glasses (between the two lenses) is a 360° vertical convex-lens camera. This unique technology enables the glasses — without requiring the user to turn their head — to simultaneously see forward and around the user up to 180° horizontally, and scan from ground level to the sky up to 180° vertically.
  • 2. Dedicated Tactile Smart Buttons on the Frame: Equipped with Quick Description Button (right frame) for instant obstacle pings, Detailed Description Button (left frame) for deep scene narration, On/Off Button, and an SOS Emergency Button that instantly sends precise live GPS coordinates along with a 360° photo to family or emergency services.
  • 3. Directional Dual Speakers & Smart Microphone: Dual directional speakers at the temple ends deliver crystal-clear audio without blocking ear canals. A compact microphone below the camera supports multilingual voice commands, with a smart separate audio system during phone calls.
  • 4. Full Smartphone Management, Internet Connectivity, and Offline Operation: Bluetooth/Wi-Fi phone integration for calls and message reading, step-by-step GPS navigation, and an internal offline AI engine for continuous obstacle scanning without mobile internet.
  • 5. Banknote, Bank Card, Purchase, and Barcode Recognition: Accurate identification of banknotes and bank cards during payment, plus barcode scanning for product prices and expiration dates.

Advanced Optical & Structural Integration

Core Technical Pillars

360° Vertical Coverage

Convex optics continuously monitor low steps and high overhead hazards in parallel without head adjustment.

Dual Perception Core

Switch between rapid obstacle pings or rich contextual environmental narration with single tactile button presses.

Open-Ear Directional Audio

Pipes crystal-clear spatial audio cues into the ear canal while keeping environmental hearing fully open.

Ergonomic Hardware Architecture

Engineered with physical tactile buttons, emergency SOS triggers, noise-canceling microphone, and a lightweight opaque frame.

Driven by Public Good Impact

Building accessible, open-source-assisted technology to deliver independence and safety at cost-price manufacturing.

Optimization of the offline/online AI core and multilingual speech recognition system.

Phase 3 (Months 7–9)

Distribution of 50 glasses among blind individuals for real-world testing in crowded urban environments and application of feedback.

Public release at minimum cost (Public Good) for comprehensive accessibility to blind individuals in need.
